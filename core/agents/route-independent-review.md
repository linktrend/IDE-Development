# Route: independent packet

The ordinary routed reviewer is the default. Luna may be selected only when the
Principal explicitly authorizes it and the packet is disjoint from all other
active ownership. Preserve exact commit/tree identity, narrow scope, and role
separation. The acceptance policy does not require a particular vendor or
model.
